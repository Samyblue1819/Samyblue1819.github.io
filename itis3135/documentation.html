Deadlock Guide

Introduction Souls Lanes Objectives Items Movement

Introduction

Deadlock is a team-based hero shooter from Valve that mixes a third-person shooter with a MOBA. Two teams of six fight across one map.

You win by destroying the enemy Patron, the giant idol at the center of their base. Everything else in a match builds toward that.

Souls

Souls are the game's only resource. You use them to level up your hero and to buy items.

  • Troopers: AI units that march down each lane and drop souls when killed
  • Deny: shoot an enemy trooper's soul orb before they can claim it
  • Soul Urn: appears after 10 minutes; carry it across the map for a team bonus

Lanes

The map has three lanes, each with troopers marching toward the enemy base. Early on, heroes split into solo and duo lanes, then group up and roam in the mid-game.

Ziplines run down every lane so heroes can get back to the fight quickly.

Objectives

Each lane has structures you must take down in order:

  • Guardian: the first tower in the lane
  • Walker: the lane boss behind the Guardian
  • Base Guardians: two towers outside the enemy base
  • Shrines: break both to weaken the Patron
  • Patron: has two phases; destroying it wins the game

Items

Items come from three shops: Weapon, Vitality, and Spirit. Each shop has four tiers that cost 800, 1,600, 3,200, and 6,400 souls.

Most items are passive. Some are Active and have to be triggered, and Imbue items attach to one of your abilities. See my Inventory page for the items I buy most.

Movement

Movement is a big part of the skill in Deadlock. Dash, slide, and dash-jump each use stamina.

Sliding right after a dash keeps your momentum. Pair movement with heroes like Wraith, whose Project Mind teleports her in or out of fights.
