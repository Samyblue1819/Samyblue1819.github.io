About

Sandy Snake Refinishing was started by Sameer Sohail, who grew up in Charlotte doing car bodywork and mechanic work. After years of fixing dents and respraying panels for friends and family, he turned the hobby into a small shop focused on paint.

We are a small crew on purpose. Instead of rushing a dozen cars through the booth each week, we take on a few at a time and give each one the hours it actually needs. You will talk to the same person from estimate to pickup.

The name comes from two things we know well: sanding, the heart of every good paint job, and snakes, which grow a fresh new skin and leave the old one behind.
