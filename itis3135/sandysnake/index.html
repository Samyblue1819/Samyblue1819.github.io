Welcome to Sandy Snake Refinishing, a small auto body shop in Charlotte that does one thing really well: bringing tired paint back to life. Faded clear coat, swirl marks, scratches, rust bubbles, or a bad respray from a previous owner, we sand it down and build it back up.

Like a snake shedding its skin, your car leaves the old, worn finish behind. What comes out is the same car you love, with paint that looks like it just left the factory, or better.

Take a look at our services, see how our process works, or stop by the shop for a free estimate.
