Services

We keep our menu short so we can do every job right. Each service starts with a free inspection so you only pay for what your paint really needs.

  • Paint Correction: machine polishing to remove swirls, haze and light scratches.
  • Spot Repair: sanding, filling and blending one damaged panel to match the rest.
  • Clear Coat Restoration: stripping peeling or faded clear and laying down a fresh coat.
  • Full Respray: a complete sand down and repaint, in the original color or a new one.
  • Ceramic Coating: a hard protective layer to keep the new finish shining.
